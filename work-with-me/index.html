One to one

A real conversation. Not a coaching session.

31 years in sales. Made redundant at 47. Left the UK with no salary and no plan beyond the decision itself.

If you are seriously thinking about leaving the UK, planning the financial side of a different life, or trying to work out if any of this is actually possible, this is the call for that conversation.

I am not a coach. I do not have a programme. I have done this and I will talk to you honestly about it.

Who this is for
  • You are thinking seriously about leaving the UK and want to talk to someone who has actually done it
  • You are planning the financial side (runway, income, investments) and want a reality check from someone living it
  • You are at the point of deciding and want one proper conversation before you commit
How it works
  1. Book and pay below. You choose a time that works for you.
  2. Send three questions in advance. Anything you want to cover. The more specific the better.
  3. We talk for 60 minutes. Honest conversation. No slides. No script.
  4. I send a short follow-up note after. Key points from the call, anything worth writing down.
What you leave with
  • A clearer picture of whether to go and what the first steps actually look like
  • Honest answers to the questions you have been sitting on. Finances, logistics, the things that feel too specific to ask publicly
  • A short follow-up note with the key points from the call, in writing, so nothing gets lost
